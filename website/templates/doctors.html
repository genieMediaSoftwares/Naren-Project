About Doctor

Dr. Naren Satya secured MBBS (2005) and MD Radiodiagnosis (2011) from Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences, bringing more than two decades of experience into his work.

He pursued specialized training in Obstetric and Fetal Ultrasound at Mediscan, one of Asia's leading centers for fetal medicine and diagnostic imaging.

Dr. Naren has performed over 3 lakh ultrasound scans and more than 10,000 image-guided procedures across multiple specialties, with a strong focus on fetal medicine.

He has also published research papers in various medical journals on ultrasound applications in thyroid disorders, fetal medicine, kidney disease, infertility and endometriosis.

Known for his patient-centered approach and attention to detail, Dr. Naren Satya focuses on providing accurate diagnosis, compassionate care and advanced fetal imaging services.
